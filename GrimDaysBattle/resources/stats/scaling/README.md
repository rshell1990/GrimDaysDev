# Opening defense requirements

These level 1–9 curves author the influencing-stat requirement for the attribute-only
maximum, not the resulting percentage and not per-point calculated-stat gains.
Harlok and both courtyard goons use the same opening requirement policy.

| Defense | Influencing attribute | Base cap | Required at level 1 / 3 / 9 |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Physical Resistance | Endurance | 20% | 40 / 50 / 80 |
| Magical Resistance | Willpower | 20% | 40 / 50 / 80 |
| Evasion | Finesse | 20% | 40 / 50 / 80 |
| Lucky Evasion | Luck | 5% | 30 / 40 / 70 |

Linear sampling is followed by the existing whole-point threshold rounding.
All four minimums are zero. These provisional caps intentionally leave high defense
to direct equipment, passive-skill and active-effect grants. Such modifiers apply
after the capped base; `maximum` must not clamp the final effective value. Bonuses
to the influencing attribute alone still obey the base cap. Chance/mitigation
consumers retain their existing 0–100% safety bounds. Critical Strike, Luck retry
rules and authored progression are unchanged.

This is an opening-tier baseline, not final pacing acceptance or a level 10–80
balance solution. Like the former opening curves, samples beyond this domain clamp
to the endpoint; later tiers need explicit authoring before they are balanced.
